MacVenom Pro - Professional Device Discovery & Directional Finding

MacVenom Pro is a professional device discovery and directional finding application for Android. It scans for nearby Bluetooth (BLE) and WiFi devices, with specialized detection for Apple devices including AirTags, iPhones, MacBooks, AirPods, and more.

Key Features​

Device Scanning​

  • Dual-Mode Scanning: Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and WiFi detection
  • Apple Device Detection: Automatically identifies AirTags, iPhones, MacBooks, Apple Watches, AirPods
  • Signal Strength Display: Shows RSSI (dBm) for each device
  • Distance Estimation: Real-time distance in feet
  • Device Type Identification: Icons and labels for device types

Custom Device Management​

  • Long-Press Renaming: Quickly rename discovered devices
  • Persistent Names: Custom names saved across app sessions
  • Easy Identification: Quickly identify frequently seen devices

Watch List​

  • Priority Monitoring: Add important devices to Watch List
  • Top Display: Watch List devices appear first in results
  • Continuous Monitoring: Keep tabs on specific devices

Directional Find (DF) Mode​

  • Signal-Based Tracking: Locate devices using RSSI strength
  • Visual Arrow Indicator: Points toward the target device
  • Real-Time Distance: Estimated distance to target
  • Confidence Percentage: Accuracy rating of bearing

Audio Proximity Beeping​

Beeping intervals based on signal strength:

  • -30 dBm or stronger: 150ms (almost there!)
  • -40 to -30 dBm: 250ms (fast)
  • -50 to -40 dBm: 400ms (medium-fast)
  • -60 to -50 dBm: 600ms (medium)
  • -70 to -60 dBm: 900ms (slow)
  • -80 to -70 dBm: 1300ms (very slow)
  • Below -80 dBm: 2000ms (distant)

Haptic Feedback​

  • Vibration Alerts: When extremely close to target
  • Triggers at -20 dBm: Indicates you're practically on top of device
  • Toggle On/Off: Enable/disable in settings

Above/Below Detection​

  • Smart Detection: Identifies when device may be on different floor
  • "Check Above or Below" Indicator: When strong signal but no clear direction
  • Triggers When: Signal is similar in all directions or device is very close

System Requirements​

  • Android 8.0 or higher
  • Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) support
  • Location services enabled
  • Valid MacVenom Pro license key
  • Internet connection (for some features)
