MacVenom Pro - Directional Finding

Directional Find (DF) Mode helps you locate a specific device by showing its bearing and using audio/haptic feedback.

Starting Directional Find​

From the Device List​

  1. Open MacVenom Pro and perform a scan
  2. Find the device you want to locate in the list
  3. Long-press on the device
  4. Select "Start Directional Find"
  5. The DF interface opens

What You'll See​

  • Compass/Arrow: Points toward the target device
  • Distance Indicator: Shows estimated distance
  • Signal Strength Bar: Visual RSSI representation
  • Confidence %: How confident the bearing is
  • Control Buttons: Audio and Haptic toggle buttons

Using the Arrow & Compass​

Understanding the Arrow​

The large arrow at the center shows the direction to your target:

  • Arrow points up: Device is in front of you
  • Arrow points down: Device is behind you
  • Arrow points left/right: Device is to that side
  • Spinning arrow: You're very close to the device
  • Blinking arrow: Lost signal momentarily

Following the Arrow​

  1. Face the direction the arrow is pointing
  2. Walk forward while keeping the arrow pointing up
  3. Listen to the beeping: Sound gets faster as you get closer
  4. Feel the vibration: If haptic is enabled
  5. Watch the distance: Gets smaller as you approach
  6. Distance says "Very Close": You're practically there!

Audio Feedback​

Beep Intervals by Distance​

The faster the beeping, the closer you are:

DistanceBeep SpeedInterval
Very Close (±5 ft)Very Fast150ms
Close (5-15 ft)Fast250ms
Medium-Close (15-30 ft)Medium-Fast400ms
Medium (30-50 ft)Medium600ms
Distant (50-100 ft)Slow900ms
Very Distant (100+ ft)Very Slow1300-2000ms

Enabling/Disabling Audio​

  1. At the bottom of the DF screen, locate the Audio button (🔊)
  2. Tap to toggle audio feedback ON/OFF
  3. Button highlights when audio is enabled

Haptic Feedback​

How Haptic Works​

When you get very close to a device (-20 dBm or stronger signal), your phone will vibrate to alert you.

  • Indicates you're practically on top of the device
  • Doesn't require sound, so works silently
  • Useful in public places where beeping isn't appropriate

Enabling/Disabling Haptic​

  1. At the bottom of the DF screen, locate the Haptic button (📳)
  2. Tap to toggle haptic feedback ON/OFF
  3. Button highlights when haptic is enabled

Testing Haptic​

  1. Enable haptic in DF Mode
  2. Get within a few feet of the target device
  3. You should feel vibration when very close

Finding Techniques​

Basic Finding​

  1. Start DF on your target device
  2. Walk forward following the arrow
  3. Listen to beeping for distance feedback
  4. Arrow points down/left/right: Device is that direction, adjust course
  5. Beeps get faster: Getting closer
  6. Very fast beeps + vibration: Device is very close

Fine-Tuning Location​

Once you're within 10 feet:

  1. Rotate 360°: Slowly turn in a circle
  2. Watch the arrow: It should stay pointing in one direction
  3. When arrow spins: You're very close and may have found it
  4. Check above/below: If no device found, device may be above or below you
  5. Look for LED lights: Many devices have indicator lights when transmitting

Above/Below Detection​

If you notice:

  • Strong beeping but can't see the device
  • Signal similar in all directions
  • Distance shows 0-5 feet but nothing found

The device may be:

  • Above you: On a shelf, ceiling, or upper floor
  • Below you: In a basement, lower floor, or under furniture

What to do:

  1. Look above your current position
  2. Then look below
  3. Try climbing stairs to upper/lower floors
  4. Move to adjacent rooms

Understanding Confidence %​

The Confidence Percentage shows how accurate the bearing is:

ConfidenceReliability
90-100%Very accurate - trust the arrow
70-89%Good - arrow is accurate
50-69%Moderate - arrow is approximate
Below 50%Low - weak signal, follow with caution

Low confidence usually means:

  • Device is weak or distant
  • Lots of signal reflection/interference
  • You're in a poor RF environment

Tips for Successful Finding​

  1. Start with Strong Signal: Begin from a location where the device shows strong signal
  2. Move Slowly: Walk slowly to maintain steady signal readings
  3. Keep Device Centered: Look at the center of your screen while moving
  4. Use Audio + Visual: Combine beeping (audio) with arrow (visual)
  5. Clear Path: Remove obstacles if possible (though signals penetrate walls)
  6. Multiple Attempts: If you lose signal, rescan and try again
  7. Turn Off Interference: Move away from WiFi routers and other radio sources if possible
  8. Open Areas Better: Searching works better in open spaces than confined areas

Troubleshooting DF​

Arrow not pointing correctly?

  • Ensure compass is calibrated (wave phone in figure-8 motion)
  • Move away from metal objects
  • Try a new DF session

Beeping stopped?

  • Device moved out of range
  • Target device may have powered off
  • Perform a new scan and rescan

Vibration not working?

  • Ensure haptic is enabled (button highlighted)
  • Check device's vibration is enabled in settings
  • Volume isn't muted

Can't find device even with strong signal?

  • Device may be hidden or disguised
  • Try checking above and below your location
  • Signal might be reflecting off buildings/hills
