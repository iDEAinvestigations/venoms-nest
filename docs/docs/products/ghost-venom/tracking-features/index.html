Ghost Venom Tracking Features

Managing Your Tags​

Creating a New Tag​

  1. Tap the "+" button on the main screen
  2. Select "Create New Tag"
  3. Give your tag a name (e.g., "My Keys", "Wallet")
  4. Choose an icon/color for easy identification
  5. Tap "Create"
  6. Ghost Venom will generate a tag ID and instructions for pairing with your BLE device

Pairing with BLE Devices​

  1. Have your BLE device ready (should be in pairing mode)
  2. In Ghost Venom, open the tag you created
  3. Tap "Pair Device"
  4. Your device will scan for nearby BLE devices
  5. Select your device from the list
  6. Confirm the pairing code if prompted
  7. Your tag is now active and tracking!

Renaming Tags​

  1. Open the tag you want to rename
  2. Tap the tag name at the top
  3. Edit the name
  4. Tap "Save"

Deleting Tags​

  1. Long-press on the tag in the main list
  2. Tap "Delete"
  3. Confirm deletion

Location Tracking​

Viewing Current Location​

  1. Open Ghost Venom's main screen
  2. You'll see all your tags with their current locations
  3. Tap any tag to see detailed location information
  4. The location is pulled from nearby Apple devices that detected your tag

Location History​

Ghost Venom stores up to 7 days of location history for each tag.

Viewing History:

  1. Open a tag
  2. Tap the "History" tab
  3. Select a date to view past locations
  4. A timeline shows all recorded locations for that day

Exporting History:

  1. Open a tag
  2. Tap "History"
  3. Tap the Export button
  4. Choose format: CSV or PDF
  5. Save to your device or cloud storage

Map Visualization​

View All Tags on Map​

  1. From the main screen, tap the "Map" tab
  2. All your active tags are displayed on the map
  3. Tap any tag icon to see its details
  4. Use Zoom In/Out to see more detail

Map Features​

  • Real-Time Updates: Tap the refresh icon to get the latest locations
  • Location History: Tap and hold a tag to see its movement history
  • Search Locations: Use the search bar to find specific addresses
  • Sharing: Long-press a location to share it via SMS or messaging apps

Real-Time Sync​

Ghost Venom keeps your tag locations up-to-date by syncing with Apple's Find My network.

Manual Sync​

  1. Open Ghost Venom
  2. Tap the Refresh icon (circular arrow) in the top right
  3. The app will fetch the latest locations from Apple's servers
  4. This typically takes 5-10 seconds per tag

Auto-Sync Settings​

  1. Open Settings
  2. Tap "Sync Preferences"
  3. Choose your sync interval:
    • Real-Time (syncs every 5 minutes)
    • Frequent (syncs every 15 minutes)
    • Standard (syncs every 30 minutes)
    • Battery Saver (syncs every 60 minutes)
note

Real-Time syncing uses more battery but provides the most accurate, up-to-date locations. Choose the option that best fits your needs.

Understanding Location Accuracy​

The accuracy of your tag's location depends on:

  • Proximity to Apple Devices: Tags are more accurate in areas with many iPhones/iPads
  • Network Coverage: Better cellular coverage = better location accuracy
  • Time Since Last Detection: Older location data may be less precise

Expected Accuracy:

  • In urban areas: Within 50-200 feet
  • In rural areas: May be less precise due to fewer Apple devices
  • Indoors: Generally accurate if near Apple devices
