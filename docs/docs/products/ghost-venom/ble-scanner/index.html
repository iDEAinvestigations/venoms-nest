Ghost Venom BLE Scanner & Device Finder

The BLE Scanner allows you to discover and identify nearby Bluetooth Low Energy devices.

Using the BLE Scanner​

Starting a Scan​

  1. From the main screen, tap the "BLE Scanner" tab
  2. The scanner will automatically begin searching for nearby BLE devices
  3. A list of detected devices will appear, sorted by signal strength

Understanding Device Information​

For each detected device, you'll see:

InformationDescription
Device NameName of the BLE device (if available)
Device TypeType of device (iPhone, AirTag, AirPods, Generic BLE, etc.)
Signal Strength (RSSI)Radio Signal Strength Indicator in dBm (closer to 0 = stronger)
DistanceEstimated distance in feet/meters
ManufacturerCompany that made the device

Device Type Indicators​

Ghost Venom automatically identifies devices with special icons:

  • 🍎 Apple Icon (Blue) - Apple device (iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch)
  • 🏷️ Tag Icon - AirTag or similar tracking tag
  • 🎧 Headphones Icon - AirPods or similar wireless earbuds
  • ❓ Generic Icon - Unknown BLE device

Directional Finder​

Once you've identified a device you want to locate, use the Directional Finder to pinpoint its location.

Starting Directional Find​

  1. From the BLE Scanner list, tap on the device you want to find
  2. Tap "Start Directional Find"
  3. The Finder interface will open

Using the Directional Finder​

The Directional Finder provides several tools to locate your device:

Visual Arrow​

  • A large arrow at the top shows the direction to your device
  • The arrow rotates as you move and turn
  • Points toward the strongest signal

Distance Indicator​

  • Shows estimated distance in feet/meters
  • Updates in real-time as you move
  • Indicates: "Very Close", "Close", "Medium", "Far"

Signal Strength Bar​

  • Visual representation of RSSI (signal strength)
  • Longer bar = stronger signal = closer to device
  • Updates 2-3 times per second

Audio Feedback​

  • Beeping that speeds up as you get closer
  • Beep intervals based on signal strength:
    • Very fast beeps (150ms) = Almost there!
    • Fast beeps (250-400ms) = Getting very close
    • Medium beeps (600ms) = Medium distance
    • Slow beeps (900-1300ms) = Still far away

Haptic Feedback​

  • Phone vibration when you get very close
  • Activates when signal is -20 dBm or stronger
  • Indicates you're practically on top of the device

Tips for Effective Finding​

  1. Move Slowly - Walk slowly while watching the arrow and listening to beeps
  2. Face the Arrow - Keep the arrow pointing away from you to move in the right direction
  3. Turn 360° - If the arrow spins, you're very close to the device
  4. Check Different Elevations - If you can't find the device, try looking above or below your current position
  5. Clear Obstacles - Metal objects and thick walls can interfere with signals
  6. Repeat Scans - Perform multiple BLE scans to ensure you're still tracking the right device

Advanced Scanner Features​

Filtering Devices​

  1. In the BLE Scanner list, tap the Filter icon
  2. Choose device types to display:
    • ☑️ Apple Devices
    • ☑️ AirTags
    • ☑️ AirPods
    • ☑️ All BLE Devices
  3. Only selected device types will appear in the scan results

Saving Device Information​

  1. Long-press on a device in the scanner list
  2. Tap "Save Device"
  3. Enter a custom name (e.g., "Friend's iPhone")
  4. Tap "Save"
  5. Your saved devices appear at the top of the list for future reference

Exporting Scan Results​

  1. From the BLE Scanner, tap the Export button
  2. Choose format: CSV or JSON
  3. Save to your device or cloud storage
  4. You can analyze results on your computer

Scanner Settings​

Access BLE Scanner settings by tapping Settings > BLE Scanner:

  • Scan Frequency: How often to scan (Every 5 sec, Every 10 sec, Every 30 sec)
  • Signal Filter: Minimum signal strength to display
  • Device Type Icons: Toggle icons on/off for cleaner display
  • Auto-Refresh: Automatically refresh scan results at intervals

Troubleshooting​

No devices appearing?

  • Ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your Android device
  • Make sure other BLE devices are powered on and in range
  • Try moving closer to the device you're searching for

Inaccurate distance readings?

  • BLE distance estimation is approximate (±50% accuracy)
  • Use the directional arrow and beeping for more precise location
  • Move to different positions for triangulation

Device keeps disappearing?

  • The target device may have moved out of range
  • Bluetooth range is typically 30-100 feet in open areas
  • Try a new scan to relocate it
