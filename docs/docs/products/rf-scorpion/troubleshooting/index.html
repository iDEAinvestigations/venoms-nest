Troubleshooting & Best Practices

Common Issues & Solutions​

App Won't Start​

Problem: RF Scorpion crashes immediately on launch

Solutions:

  1. Check storage space - Need at least 200MB free
  2. Clear app cache:
    • Settings > Apps > RF Scorpion > Storage > Clear Cache
  3. Clear app data (resets settings but keeps license):
    • Settings > Apps > RF Scorpion > Storage > Clear Data
  4. Uninstall and reinstall the app
  5. Restart your device and try again

License Won't Activate​

Problem: "Invalid License" or "Activation Failed" error

Solutions:

  1. Check Internet Connection: License activation requires internet
  2. Verify License Key: Ensure you copied it correctly (case-sensitive)
  3. Try Again Later: Activation servers may be temporarily down
  4. Check Expiration: Some licenses have start/end dates
  5. Contact Support: sales@ideatech.us with your license key and device info

If Still Failing:

  • Try activating via Wi-Fi instead of cellular
  • Try in a different location
  • Clear app data and try fresh activation
  • Contact Venom's Nest support team

Permissions Not Working​

Problem: App asks for permissions repeatedly or says permissions denied

Solutions:

  1. Open Settings > Apps > RF Scorpion > Permissions

  2. For each permission:

    • Tap the permission name
    • Select "Allow"
    • Confirm selection

  3. Required permissions:

    • ✅ Location - For tower and Wi-Fi scanning
    • ✅ Bluetooth - For tracker detection
    • ✅ Phone State - For cellular information
    • ✅ Microphone - For audio bug detection
    • ✅ Wi-Fi - For network scanning

  4. Restart RF Scorpion after enabling permissions

tip

If permissions won't stay enabled, you may have an app permission manager. Check Settings > Apps > Special app access for any third-party permission controllers.

Location Services Issues​

Problem: Tower Survey won't detect towers or shows no results

Solutions:

  1. Enable Location Services:
    • Open Settings > Location
    • Toggle Location ON
    • Select High Accuracy mode
  2. Check Location Permission:
    • Settings > Apps > RF Scorpion > Permissions > Location
    • Set to "Allow all the time" (not just "while using app")
  3. Improve GPS Signal:
    • Move outdoors away from buildings
    • Remove phone from case if metallic
    • Wait 30+ seconds for GPS lock
  4. Toggle Location Off/On:
    • Turn off location
    • Wait 10 seconds
    • Turn back on
    • Retry scanning

Bluetooth Scanning Shows No Devices​

Problem: SCAN BLUETOOTH returns no results even though devices are nearby

Solutions:

  1. Enable Bluetooth:
    • Open Settings > Bluetooth
    • Toggle Bluetooth ON
  2. Check Bluetooth Permission:
    • Settings > Apps > RF Scorpion > Permissions > Bluetooth
    • Must be set to "Allow"
  3. Make Devices Discoverable:
    • Put target Bluetooth devices in pairing mode
    • Devices must be actively broadcasting
  4. Restart Bluetooth:
    • Turn off Bluetooth
    • Wait 10 seconds
    • Turn back on
    • Retry scan
  5. Check Hardware:
    • Open GPS Trackers & Skimmers tab
    • Check Hardware Status Panel
    • If Bluetooth shows ❌, hardware not working

EMF Readings Seem Wrong​

Problem: EMF readings are unusually high or don't match expectations

Solutions:

  1. Check Location:
    • EMF is higher near power lines, transformers, microwave ovens
    • Move to different location and compare
  2. Turn Off Other Devices:
    • Wi-Fi routers produce significant EMF
    • Cell towers nearby increase readings
    • Unplug electronics to reduce interference
  3. Check Phone Sensors:
    • Different phones have different sensor sensitivity
    • Older phones may have less accurate sensors
    • Try baseline comparison in known low-EMF area
  4. Device-Specific Issues:
    • Some Android versions have sensor issues
    • Check if your phone has known magnetometer problems
    • Update Android OS if available

Tower Scan Results Are Inconsistent​

Problem: Running the same scan in the same location shows different results

Solutions:

  1. This is Normal: Tower signals change constantly based on:
    • Network load
    • Weather conditions
    • Time of day
    • Building interference
  2. For Baselines: Run multiple scans and average results
  3. For Comparisons: Run scans at same time each day
  4. Check Movement: Even small movements change signal strength
  5. Wait Between Scans: Allow 5 minutes between scans for network stabilization

Audio Bug Scan Shows Many Detections​

Problem: Audio bug scan reports too many potential bugs

Solutions:

  1. Reduce Noise:
    • Turn off Wi-Fi if not needed
    • Turn off Bluetooth
    • Move away from electrical wiring
    • Move away from windows
  2. False Positive Check:
    • Many detections in normal buildings expected
    • Low confidence detections likely false positives
    • Focus on "Very High" confidence results only
  3. Manual Verification:
    • Visually inspect areas with high confidence alerts
    • Look for actual suspicious devices
    • Photo and identify any suspicious objects
  4. Change Location:
    • Test in different room
    • Compare results across locations
    • Consistent issues suggest environmental interference

Performance Tips​

Speed Up Scans​

  • Close Other Apps: Background apps drain resources
  • Clear Recent Apps: Swipe recent apps closed
  • Restart Phone: Fresh start improves performance
  • Free Up Storage: Keep 500MB+ free for optimal performance
  • Disable Other Hardware: Turn off Wi-Fi/Bluetooth when not scanning

Improve Accuracy​

Tower Survey:

  • Stay still during scan
  • Move 100+ feet away between scans
  • Scan at consistent times for comparison
  • Use same location for baseline

Bluetooth Scanning:

  • Scan in open areas
  • Move slowly through space
  • Rescan multiple times for confirmation
  • Note signal trends, not single readings

EMF Detection:

  • Turn off nearby electronics
  • Avoid power lines and transformers
  • Scan multiple times at same location
  • Create baseline for comparison

Best Practices & Safety​

Regular Security Routine​

Monthly:

  • Full scan of home with all features
  • Document baseline readings
  • Check for new suspicious devices

When Traveling:

  • Scan hotel room on arrival
  • Check vehicle before driving
  • Scan luggage areas
  • Scan workspace if applicable

Before Important Events:

  • Tower survey of meeting location
  • Wi-Fi scan for rogue networks
  • EMF baseline before meeting
  • Audio bug check

Documentation​

Keep records of:

  • 📸 Screenshots of baseline readings
  • 📍 Locations scanned
  • 📅 Dates and times of scans
  • 📊 Trends over time
  • 🚩 Any suspicious findings

Reporting Suspicious Activity​

If you find evidence of surveillance:

  1. Document Everything:

    • Photos of suspicious devices
    • Screenshots of scan results
    • Exact location information
    • Time and date found

  2. Preserve Evidence:

    • Do not touch or move objects
    • Do not disassemble devices
    • Note surroundings
    • Create detailed descriptions

  3. Report to Authorities:

    • Local law enforcement
    • FBI (if national security concern)
    • Your employer's security team
    • Building management/owner

Legal Considerations​

⚠️ Legal Usage:

  • ✅ Scan your own property
  • ✅ Use for personal security
  • ✅ Professional security assessments (with authorization)
  • ✅ Law enforcement investigations (with legal authority)

❌ Illegal Usage:

  • ❌ Scan others' property without permission
  • ❌ Unauthorized surveillance detection in restricted areas
  • ❌ Interfere with authorized equipment
  • ❌ Use for stalking or harassment

Advanced Troubleshooting​

Clear All App Data (Nuclear Option)​

If nothing else works, completely reset the app:

  1. Backup Important Info:

    • Take screenshots of any saved data
    • Note your license key

  2. Remove App:

    • Long-press RF Scorpion icon
    • Tap Uninstall
    • Confirm removal

  3. Clear Remnants:

    • Settings > Apps > Show system apps
    • Search "RF Scorpion" or "Venom"
    • If found, tap > Storage > Clear Cache

  4. Reinstall Fresh:

    • Download APK again
    • Install clean copy
    • Reactivate license

Contact Support​

If issues persist:

  • Email: sales@ideatech.us
  • Phone: 708-808-0719
  • Website: www.ideatech.us

Include in Support Request:

  • Your license key
  • Device model and Android version
  • Description of the problem
  • Steps you've already tried
  • Screenshots of errors
