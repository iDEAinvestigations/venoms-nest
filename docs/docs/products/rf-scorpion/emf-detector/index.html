EMF Detector & Audio Bug Detection

RF Scorpion includes tools for detecting electromagnetic fields (EMF) and hidden microphones used for audio surveillance.

EMF Detector - Electromagnetic Field Detection​

What is EMF?​

EMF (Electromagnetic Field) is radiation produced by electronic devices:

  • Sources: Phones, Wi-Fi routers, power lines, hidden surveillance devices
  • Safety Concern: High EMF exposure may be hazardous
  • Detection Value: Unexpected EMF hotspots may indicate hidden equipment

How EMF Detection Works​

RF Scorpion uses your phone's sensors to measure electromagnetic radiation levels:

  • Measurement Unit: Milligauss (mG) or Tesla (T)
  • Typical Background: 0.5-2 mG
  • High Risk: Above 5 mG continuously

Opening EMF Detector​

  1. From main menu, tap EMF Detector tab
  2. Real-time EMF measurement displays
  3. Readings update continuously as you move

Understanding EMF Readings​

Reading (mG)LevelMeaning
0-2LowNormal background radiation
2-5MediumElevated, but normal near electronics
5-10HighPotential concern
10+Very HighSignificant EMF source nearby

Hotspot Identification​

The goal is to locate EMF hotspots:

  1. Get Baseline: Record normal EMF in safe area
  2. Scan Area: Move around slowly
  3. Identify Spikes: Note locations where reading increases
  4. Map Hotspots: Mark high EMF areas on map
  5. Investigate Sources: Find what's producing the field

Common EMF Sources​

Legitimate Sources:

  • 🔌 Electrical outlets and wiring
  • 📱 Smartphones and chargers
  • 🛜 Wi-Fi routers and base stations
  • 📺 Television and microwave ovens
  • 🔋 Laptop chargers and power supplies
  • 💡 Fluorescent lights

Suspicious Sources:

  • ⚠️ Hidden devices producing unexpected EMF
  • ⚠️ Surveillance equipment in walls
  • ⚠️ Hidden listening devices
  • ⚠️ Tracking devices with RF components

Risk Assessment​

Low Risk Indicators:

  • ✅ EMF clustered near known electronics
  • ✅ Consistent readings when moving away
  • ✅ Expected sources (lights, outlets)

High Risk Indicators:

  • ❌ Unexplained EMF hotspots
  • ❌ EMF in unusual locations
  • ❌ Readings spike in specific spots
  • ❌ Persistent high readings in closed areas

Real-Time Graphing​

EMF Detector shows:

  • Live Graph: EMF levels over time
  • Peak Detection: Highest readings shown
  • Trend Analysis: See if levels rising/falling
  • Historical Data: View past measurements

To Use Graph:

  1. Start EMF scan
  2. Watch graph update in real-time
  3. Observe peaks and valleys
  4. Note timing of high readings
  5. Correlate with your movement

Audio Bug Detection (NEW in v2.0)​

What is Audio Surveillance?​

Hidden microphones or audio recording devices used for:

  • Eavesdropping on conversations
  • Recording sensitive information
  • Corporate espionage
  • Unauthorized surveillance

Detection Methods​

RF Scorpion detects audio bugs through:

Microphone Detection:

  • Identifies hidden microphones
  • Detects microphone power signatures
  • Locates active recording devices

Audio Frequency Analysis:

  • Analyzes RF audio transmission
  • Detects wireless audio transmission
  • Identifies frequency bands used

Bug Classification:

  • Determines device type
  • Identifies surveillance equipment
  • Assesses threat level

How to Scan for Audio Bugs​

1. Open Audio Bugs Tab

  • From main menu, tap Audio Bugs tab

2. Start Scan

  • Tap "SCAN ROOM" button
  • App scans for:
    • Microphone components
    • Audio transmission frequencies
    • Recording devices
    • Wireless microphones

3. Review Results

Results show:

  • Detected Devices: Any microphones found
  • Device Type: Classification of equipment
  • Location Heatmap: Where in room device detected
  • Confidence Level: How certain detection is
  • Frequency: RF frequency if wireless

Interpreting Results​

ConfidenceMeaningAction
Very High (95%+)Almost certainly a bugInvestigate immediately
High (75-95%)Likely audio bugInspect location carefully
Medium (50-75%)Possible bugManual inspection needed
Low (Below 50%)UncertainLikely false positive

Location Heatmap​

The heatmap shows:

  • 🔴 Red Areas - Highest probability bug location
  • 🟡 Yellow Areas - Moderate probability
  • 🟢 Green Areas - Lower probability

To Use Heatmap:

  1. Review color zones
  2. Red zones are most likely bug locations
  3. Physically inspect red zone areas
  4. Look for unusual devices or components

Recording Detection​

The app can detect:

  • ✅ Active audio recording
  • ✅ Microphone activation patterns
  • ✅ Recording device indicators
  • ✅ Audio transmission signals

How Recording Detection Works:

  1. Monitors for microphone power usage
  2. Detects characteristic RF patterns
  3. Identifies recording frequency
  4. Alerts if recording starts

Manual Inspection After Scan​

If app detects possible bugs:

Common Hidden Camera Locations:

  • 📌 Smoke detectors
  • 🕐 Clock radios
  • 🖼️ Picture frames
  • 💡 Light fixtures
  • 🪲 Air vents
  • 🔌 Electrical outlets
  • 🎧 Speaker systems
  • 📷 Decorative objects

Visual Inspection Checklist:

  • Check for extra wires
  • Look for small holes or lenses
  • Inspect for unusual weight in objects
  • Check for fresh construction/modifications
  • Look for objects facing seating areas
  • Check behind mirrors and glass
  • Examine electronics for modifications

Best Practices​

Regular Scanning​

  • Home: Weekly baseline check
  • Work: Before sensitive meetings
  • Hotels: Upon arrival in room
  • Conference Rooms: Before confidential meetings
  • Vehicles: Monthly security checks

Documentation​

When detecting suspicious signals:

  1. Take screenshots of readings
  2. Note exact location
  3. Record time and date
  4. Document all visual findings
  5. Preserve evidence for authorities

Safety Precautions​

  • ⚠️ Do not disassemble unknown devices - May be dangerous
  • ⚠️ Do not touch suspicious objects - May be evidence
  • ⚠️ Avoid signal interference - Turn off other devices for cleaner scan
  • ⚠️ Use in secure location - Scanning may alert device operators

Multiple Scans​

  • Scan multiple times for confirmation
  • Scan at different times of day
  • Move slowly through area for accuracy
  • Use graph data for trending

Limitations​

⚠️ Phone Sensors

  • Sensitivity varies by phone model
  • Some phones have better EMF sensors
  • Sensor placement varies

⚠️ Frequency Coverage

  • Can detect common microphone frequencies
  • May miss devices on exotic frequencies
  • Shielded devices harder to detect

⚠️ False Positives

  • Legitimate electronics may trigger detection
  • Wi-Fi routers produce RF signals
  • Cell towers nearby affect readings

⚠️ Detection Range

  • Range depends on device power
  • Weak devices harder to detect
  • Shielding reduces detection range

Troubleshooting​

Scan finds high EMF everywhere:

  • This is normal in urban areas
  • Power lines and networks create background EMF
  • Compare to baseline in different location
  • Focus on unexpected hotspots

Audio bug scan has high false positive rate:

  • Move to quieter location (reduces interference)
  • Turn off other wireless devices
  • Restart app and rescan
  • Use manual inspection to confirm findings

Readings seem inaccurate:

  • Different phones have different sensor quality
  • Sensor may need calibration
  • Try scanning in known low-EMF area
  • Compare results with professional equipment if critical
