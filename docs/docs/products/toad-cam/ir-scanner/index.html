IR Camera Scanner - Detecting Hidden Cameras with Infrared

The IR Camera Scanner is the core feature of ToadCAM that helps detect hidden cameras in rooms and spaces using your phone's infrared capabilities.

How It Works​

Most hidden cameras use infrared (IR) LEDs for night vision:

  • Humans can't see IR: But smartphone cameras can detect it
  • IR LEDs appear bright: On your phone screen as glowing spots
  • Camera lenses reflect: Flash and IR filters make them obvious

Why IR Works​

  • Hidden cameras in smoke detectors, clocks, USB chargers all use IR
  • IR LEDs glow brightly when viewed through camera
  • Most phone cameras are naturally IR-sensitive
  • Even in daylight, IR LEDs are detectable

Opening the Scanner​

From Main Menu:

  1. Tap "START SCAN" (green button)
  2. Live camera feed appears
  3. You'll see the room in normal view

Control Buttons at Bottom:

  • FILTER - Change IR detection mode
  • FLASH - Toggle strobe light
  • AI - Enable AI detection
  • SNAPSHOT - Capture findings
  • X - Close scanner

Filter Modes​

The FILTER button cycles through different viewing modes. Tap it repeatedly to cycle.

Filter: OFF (Normal View)​

  • Standard camera view
  • No special effects
  • Good for getting oriented
  • Shows what you see with naked eye

When to use: Getting baseline view, general room survey

Filter: RED (IR with Flash)​

  • Best used WITH strobe flash enabled
  • Hidden cameras appear as bright reflections
  • Background appears reddish
  • Camera lenses create obvious bright spots

How to use:

  1. Tap FILTER until "RED" shows
  2. Tap FLASH to enable strobe
  3. Point at suspicious areas
  4. Camera lenses will glow and flash

Best for: Detecting pinhole cameras, lens reflections

Filter: NV GREEN (Night Vision Green)​

  • Displays in green phosphor night vision style
  • IR sources appear very bright
  • Good for low-light scanning
  • Creates contrast between room and IR LEDs

How to use:

  1. Tap FILTER until "NV GREEN" shows
  2. Scan the room slowly
  3. IR LEDs appear as bright green spots
  4. Works in daylight or darkness

Best for: General camera detection, low-light areas

Filter: PURPLE (Direct IR Detection)​

  • Direct IR LED detection mode
  • Active IR lights appear very bright
  • Camera IR illuminators glow
  • Best for night vision cameras

How to use:

  1. Tap FILTER until "PURPLE" shows
  2. IR LEDs appear almost white/bright
  3. Scan walls, furniture, fixtures
  4. Active cameras easier to spot

Best for: Night vision cameras, active IR detection

Filter: LENS (Lens Reflection Mode)​

  • High contrast black and white mode
  • Emphasizes lens reflections
  • Glass surfaces create bright reflections
  • Camera lenses stand out clearly

How to use:

  1. Tap FILTER until "LENS" shows
  2. All glass surfaces become reflective
  3. Scan room surfaces
  4. Any camera lens will create distinct reflection

Best for: Finding cameras behind glass, lens detection

Flash Strobe Feature​

The FLASH button enables strobe light:

  • Rapidly cycles phone's flash on/off
  • Creates distinctive reflection from camera lenses
  • Best paired with RED filter
  • Highly effective for detection

How to use:

  1. Tap FLASH button (toggles on/off)
  2. Flash will strobe rapidly
  3. Point at suspicious areas
  4. Camera lenses reflect flash brightly
  5. Normal objects absorb flash light

Flash + Red Filter Combination:

  • Most effective detection method
  • Camera lenses create obvious bright flashes
  • Works even in daylight
  • Highest success rate for finding cameras
tip

Flash strobe + RED filter is the most reliable combination for camera detection. Start with this setup.

AI Detection Feature​

The AI button enables automatic camera detection:

  • AI analyzes video for camera shapes
  • Detection boxes appear around suspected cameras
  • Shows confidence percentage (higher = more certain)
  • Phone vibrates when camera detected

How to use:

  1. Tap AI button to enable
  2. Scan the room normally
  3. Watch for red/yellow detection boxes
  4. Boxes appear around suspected cameras
  5. Phone vibrates at detection

Confidence Levels:

ConfidenceMeaningAction
95%+Almost certainly a cameraInspect immediately
75-95%Likely a cameraCheck carefully
50-75%Possible cameraManual inspection
Below 50%UncertainProbably false positive

Tips for AI Detection:

  • Works best with good lighting
  • Helps identify camera-shaped objects
  • Supplement with manual inspection
  • Can have false positives on buttons, circles, etc.

Snapshot Feature​

The SNAPSHOT button captures the current view:

How to use:

  1. Find a suspicious area
  2. Frame it in the viewfinder
  3. Tap SNAPSHOT button
  4. Image is saved to photo gallery
  5. Use for documentation/evidence

Why take snapshots:

  • Document suspected cameras
  • Create evidence file
  • Share with security team
  • Review later for patterns

Where images are saved:

  • Android photo gallery
  • Accessible through Photos app
  • Can share directly from gallery

Scanning Technique​

Best Practices for Room Scanning​

1. Preparation:

  • Turn off overhead lights (or dim them)
  • Ensure you have good battery
  • Have time to scan thoroughly (10-15 min per room)

2. Scanner Setup:

  • Enable FLASH (strobe mode)
  • Select RED filter
  • Enable AI detection if available
  • Ensure you have good phone positioning

3. Scanning Sequence:

  • Start from doorway
  • Move systematically through room
  • Scan each wall section
  • Don't miss high places (ceilings, corners)
  • Don't miss low places (baseboards, floor)

4. Focus Areas:

  • Smoke detectors (very common hiding spot)
  • Light fixtures and bulbs
  • Clock radios and electronic devices
  • Picture frames
  • Air vents and grilles
  • Decorative objects
  • Electrical outlets
  • Mirrors and glass
  • Bookshelves and items
  • Bathroom fixtures

Detailed Room Checklist​

Bedroom:

  • Nightstand items
  • Light switches and outlets
  • Ceiling fixtures
  • Closet interior
  • Behind mirrors
  • Under bed (with mirror/camera pointed at bed)

Bathroom:

  • Exhaust fan
  • Light fixtures
  • Mirror and frame
  • Soap dispenser
  • Towel racks
  • Toilet area
  • Shower head/curtain

Living Room:

  • Television and stand
  • Bookshelves
  • Wall decorations
  • Coffee table items
  • Lamps and bulbs
  • Remote control
  • Picture frames

Office/Workspace:

  • Computer monitor
  • Desk phone
  • Pen holders
  • Wall decorations
  • Smoke detector
  • Vents
  • Cable boxes

Understanding Common Hidden Cameras​

Pinhole Cameras​

  • Tiny camera lens (1-2mm hole)
  • Can be hidden in almost anything
  • Very hard to spot with naked eye
  • IR filter shows them clearly

Detection Method: RED + FLASH filter, slow scan

Smoke Detector Cameras​

  • Most common hiding spot
  • Camera behind detector lens
  • Very professional looking
  • Almost impossible to notice without IR

Detection Method: Point scanner directly at detector, look for lens

Clock Radio Cameras​

  • Camera hidden in alarm clock
  • Front-facing toward bed
  • Lens may be visible if you know to look
  • Common in hotel rooms

Detection Method: Scan clock face directly, look for extra holes

USB Charger Cameras​

  • Camera in power adapter
  • Points out of charging port or hole
  • Can charge phone while recording
  • Common in budget hotels

Detection Method: Inspect chargers carefully, IR scan any holes

Pen/Object Cameras​

  • Tiny camera in pen, button, or small object
  • Can be placed anywhere
  • Very hard to detect visually
  • IR very effective

Detection Method: Scan all small objects, look for lens reflections

NFC Detection​

If your phone has NFC (Near Field Communication):

  • App automatically scans for NFC tags
  • Some cameras have NFC tags for activation
  • If detected:
    • Phone vibrates with pattern
    • Dialog shows tag information
    • You can copy the tag ID

Common Detection Mistakes​

False Positives​

These are NOT hidden cameras:

  • ❌ Phone charging cables (end looks like lens)
  • ❌ Buttons on furniture (round and reflective)
  • ❌ Screw holes (small round openings)
  • ❌ Air vent holes (grid pattern)
  • ❌ Decorative objects with holes

How to confirm:

  • Does it have actual glass lens?
  • Can you see camera components?
  • Does it move or have wires?

Issues with Detection​

Too many false positives:

  • Turn off FLASH if too sensitive
  • Switch to different filter
  • Disable AI detection if too aggressive
  • Focus on manual inspection

Can't find anything:

  • Try different filters
  • Ensure good lighting
  • Scan more slowly
  • Check less obvious hiding spots

After Finding Something Suspicious​

If you find what you believe is a hidden camera:

  1. Don't touch it - Preserve evidence
  2. Document it:
    • Take multiple photos
    • Note exact location
    • Record room name/number
    • Note date and time
  3. Inform authorities:
    • Hotel management (if in hotel)
    • Building owner/manager
    • Local police
    • FBI (if serious)

Troubleshooting Scanner​

Scanner shows very dark image:

  • Your room is too dark
  • Point toward light source
  • Turn on lights for scanning
  • Enable filters for better visibility

Flash doesn't seem to work:

  • Flash may be already on (constant light)
  • Tap again to cycle modes
  • Check if phone's flash hardware working
  • Try disabling and re-enabling

AI detection not working:

  • AI may not be supported on your phone
  • Update ToadCAM to latest version
  • Ensure good processing power available
  • Close other apps

Camera very blurry:

  • Phone needs to focus
  • Move closer/farther from object
  • Tap on screen to refocus
  • Wait a moment for autofocus
